A hub and dropout spacing standard that widens the front axle to 110 mm and rear to 148 mm (from 100/142 mm), pushing the chainline out for shorter chainstays and stiffer wheels.

Boost affects axle standard strings, chainring offset, and brake rotor alignment — "Boost" in a wheel spec is not cosmetic; non-Boost wheels generally do not fit Boost frames without new hubs.
